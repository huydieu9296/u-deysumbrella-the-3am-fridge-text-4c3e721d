## Entry

See [`entry.txt`](entry.txt) — the full text, verbatim:

> 3AM again. I've kept your ice cream at a perfect -18°C and your life choices at room temperature. Please go to bed.

Why it works: only a fridge talks in temperatures — the ice cream is a perfect -18°C, but the owner's life choices are stuck at room temperature. It's the appliance that has been awake all night, gently roasting its owner for the third 3AM raid in a row.
